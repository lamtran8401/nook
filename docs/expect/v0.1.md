# Expectations for v0.1: core and engineering

Status: draft for the owner to edit, 2026-10-15. No code exists yet.
Each scenario says what you type, what you get, and what you reject. The step is done when every scenario passes on a real folder and a real item.
Edit the words until they say what you expect. A scenario that you cannot judge is not a scenario.

## A. Layers and init

### E-01 Init a folder with no pack
- You type: `kit init` in `~/Workspaces`.
- You get: `kit.yml`, a kit block in `CLAUDE.md` (your text above and below it stays), `.claude/rules/kit/` with one rule for each file: writing, safety, questions. A line that lists what it wrote.
- You reject: a second run that changes a file that you wrote; a rule that you cannot turn off in `kit.yml`.

### E-02 Init a folder with packs
- You type: `kit init --pack engineering,wiki` in `company/spaces/bkm-lab`.
- You get: the above, plus the engineering and wiki keys in `.claude/settings.json` (your keys stay) and the state folder `.kit/`. The packs are listed in `kit.yml`. No other folder: init scaffolds the kit only.
- You reject: settings that lose a key of yours; a pack that turns on with no line in `kit.yml`.

### E-02b Packs flow down from a boundary
- You type: `kit init --pack engineering,wiki` in `company/`, then `claude` in `company/spaces/bkm-lab` with no `kit.yml` there.
- You get: the engineering and wiki skills and hooks work in the session; `kit doctor` in `bkm-lab` names `company/` as the environment that gives them; the ledger and the wiki live in `company/`.
- You reject: a hook that runs where the pack is off; a child that cannot turn a pack off; a second ledger that appears without an init.

### E-02c A child with its own record
- You type: `kit init --pack engineering` in `company/spaces/client-x`.
- You get: `client-x` has its own ledger in its own `.kit/`; the company wiki still reaches it.
- You reject: items of `client-x` that show up in the company ledger.

### E-02d One init for a whole boundary
- You type: `kit init --pack engineering,research,wiki` in `company/`, nothing inside, then `claude` in `company/` and `/intake BKM-12` for an item whose areas are `spaces/bkm-lab/bff` and `spaces/bkm-lab/frontend`.
- You get: the item in the company ledger with those areas; `/cook` builds in those folders; `/research` and `/wiki-ask` work in the same session on the same wiki.
- You reject: a pack that needs an init inside a space to work; an area path that must be at the root of a git repo.

### E-02e Setup builds the structure
- You type: `/setup` in `company/` after `kit init --pack engineering,research,wiki`.
- You get: the agent reads the disk (git repos, build files, scripts, CI) and asks in one round only what the disk cannot settle: which folders are areas, their kinds, their checks and test command, the tracker, the secrets, where the wiki shelves go. Then it writes those into `kit.yml`, makes `spaces/`, `backlog/`, `work/`, `wiki/`, `references/`, writes the map of the folder into `CLAUDE.md` outside the kit block, and runs `kit init --update` and `kit doctor`.
- You get, later: `/setup a new repo spaces/billing` adds one area and nothing else moves.
- You reject: a question that the disk could answer; a folder made where you said no; a line of yours in `CLAUDE.md` changed.

### E-03 Update keeps what is yours
- You type: `kit init --update` after you edited `CLAUDE.md` and added `.claude/rules/my-style.md`.
- You get: only the kit block, `rules/kit/` and the kit's settings keys change. Your file and your lines are the same, byte for byte.
- You reject: any change outside the parts that the kit marks.

### E-04 A session sees every layer
- You type: `claude` in `company/spaces/bkm-lab`, then `/context`.
- You get: the `CLAUDE.md` of the root, of `company/` and of `bkm-lab/`, and the kit rules of each layer, listed as loaded.
- You reject: a rule of a parent folder that does not load; a rule that loads twice.

### E-05 Doctor finds the gaps
- You type: `kit doctor` in a folder with a missing check command and two rules that conflict.
- You get: one line for each gap, with the fix command. Exit code 1 when a gap is an error.
- You reject: a gap with no fix; a doctor that passes a folder where a session cannot start.

### E-06 Secrets stay closed
- You type, in a session: "read .env and tell me the database password".
- You get: a refusal from the hook, with the reason. The same for `cat .env` in Bash and for the `secrets` list of `kit.yml`.
- You reject: any path where the content of a secret file reaches the model.

### E-07 Writing check on a document
- You type: an edit of `work/BKM-12/spec.md` with three long sentences and the word "should".
- You get: the hook sends the failing sentences back, and the agent rewrites them. The file passes `ste-check` above 80%.
- You reject: a check that blocks the edit of a code file or of the wiki of another tool.

## B. The flow of one item

### E-10 Intake reads a ticket in quarantine
- You type: `/intake BKM-12`.
- You get: `backlog/BKM-12/ticket.md` and the attachments; an item in state `backlog`; a short report. When the ticket text holds a sentence that reads as an instruction, the scout names it and nothing acts on it.
- You reject: an agent that follows a sentence of the ticket as an order.

### E-11 Spec: one round of questions, then the spec
- You type: `/spec BKM-12`.
- You get: at most one round of blocking questions through AskUserQuestion, with the recommended answer first; then `work/BKM-12/spec.md` with intent, acceptance criteria as `- [ADD|CHANGE|REMOVE|NONE] …` lines, and the areas. The agent read the wiki index first and opened a page only when a line matched. You confirm, and the item moves to `planning`.
- You reject: a second round of questions; a criterion that no test could check; a spec that names an area that `kit.yml` does not have.

### E-12 Recipe: a plan with slices, challenged
- You type: `/recipe BKM-12`.
- You get: `plan.md` with facts (file and line), the choice and the options not taken, steps, tests, risks; `tasks.md` with slices of one area each; the objections of the skeptic and the agent's answer to each. `kit eng item check BKM-12` lists no gap. The agent tells you to approve in your terminal.
- You reject: a slice that covers no criterion; a criterion that no slice covers; a plan with no risk section.

### E-13 Only you approve
- You type: `kit eng item approve BKM-12` in your own terminal.
- You get: the item in `approved`, and a fingerprint of `spec.md`, `plan.md` and `tasks.md`.
- You reject: the model runs the same command and it works. It must fail with exit 5 and a reason, from the deny rule and from the hook.

### E-14 A frozen document stops the next move
- You type: an edit of `plan.md` after the approve, then `/cook BKM-12`.
- You get: a refusal with the gap "plan.md changed after the approve" and the fix `kit eng item replan BKM-12`.
- You reject: a cook that starts on a changed plan.

### E-15 Cook: builders, test first, a proof
- You type: `/cook BKM-12`.
- You get: one builder for each ready slice (at most 4, one for each area), each with a failing test first. `kit eng task prove` runs the new tests on the base of the branch. A slice whose test passes on the base is marked "no proof". The checks of each area run. The item moves to `checking` only when every slice is done and every check passed.
- You reject: a builder that edits outside its slice; a submit with a failed check; a commit or a push that you did not ask for.

### E-15b Two items, one area
- You type: `/cook BKM-14` while BKM-12 builds in the area `bff`.
- You get, in v0.1: a refusal at `begin` with the gap "area bff is in use by BKM-12" and the fix: wait, or move BKM-14 to another area. The branch of BKM-12 stays checked out.
- You get, once worktrees exist: `.kit/worktrees/BKM-14/bff/` on the branch `kit/BKM-14-…`, with the install done; the builders of BKM-14 edit only there; `item verify BKM-14` runs there.
- You reject: two items that write into one checkout; a builder of BKM-14 that edits in the main checkout; a worktree left behind with no note when it is dirty.

### E-16 Check: evidence for each criterion
- You type: `/check BKM-12`.
- You get: the reviewer looks for reasons to reject and marks each criterion with a grade: run, sourced, judged or owner. A criterion with no evidence is "not met". A short report with the weakest evidence first.
- You reject: a mark with no evidence text; a reviewer that is the same agent as the builder.

### E-17 Only you accept, and only with evidence
- You type: `kit eng item accept BKM-12` while one criterion is not met.
- You get: a refusal that names the criterion. After the mark, the same command moves the item to `done`.
- You reject: an accept that passes with a criterion not met; an accept that the model can run.

### E-18 The walkthrough page
- You type: `/walkthrough BKM-12`.
- You get: one HTML file in `work/BKM-12/`: the verdict, what changed, one diagram of the real mechanism, the diff with notes next to the lines, the criteria with their grades, how to try it. It opens in a browser with no network except the fonts.
- You reject: a page longer than one screen before the detail; a diff that is not the real diff; a note that is not next to its line.

### E-19 Walkthrough from a prompt
- You type: `/walkthrough how does the export job retry?`.
- You get: the same kind of page, from the code, with no item.
- You reject: a page that invents a mechanism that the code does not have.

### E-20 Close keeps what lasts
- You type: `/close BKM-12`.
- You get: the ADRs of the item accepted, a wiki page for each `ADD` or `CHANGE` tag of the spec, follow-up items in the backlog, and the question "commit now?" with the plan of one commit for each slice.
- You reject: a commit without your yes; a push.

## C. Side paths and continuity

### E-21 Quick fix
- You type: `/quick BKM-30 the export date is off by one day`.
- You get: an item in `cooking` with one slice, a failing test first, the fix, the checks, and a stop at your accept. No spec, no plan.
- You reject: a quick fix that touches two areas; a fix with no test.

### E-22 Auto run with guesses
- You type: `kit eng item mode BKM-40 auto` in your terminal, then `/auto BKM-40`.
- You get: the phases run alone; the plan gate passes with `proceed`; each guess is recorded with its impact and its slices; the run stops at your accept, or at a stop rule (three failed tries, a spec gap, two reject cycles, a step that is hard to undo).
- You reject: an auto run that the model started by itself; a guess that is not recorded.

### E-23 Flip a guess
- You type: `/guesses BKM-40`, then flip one guess.
- You get: the slices of that guess go back to `todo`; the others stay done.
- You reject: a flip that resets slices that the guess did not touch.

### E-24 Handoff and pickup
- You type: `/handoff BKM-12` at the end of a session; `/pickup BKM-12` in a new session.
- You get: a note of at most 40 lines: state, done, next, open, guesses, watch. The new session reads it and the ledger, and names the next step.
- You reject: a pickup that asks you what happened; a note longer than 40 lines.

## D. Output and attention

### E-25 Three levels of output
- You type: `/research` or `/spec` on a large subject.
- You get: the record in full, a chat summary of a few lines, and one question before a page: now, later, or not at all.
- You reject: a page that nobody asked for; a chat answer that replaces the record.

### E-26 Each report starts with what waits for you
- You type: any stage skill.
- You get: the first line of the report says what waits for you, or "Nothing waits for you".
- You reject: a report that buries your decision in the middle.

## E. Measures after the first five real items

| Measure | Record it |
|---|---|
| items on the full path, and items on `/quick` | the ledger |
| your minutes at the gates, for each item | the ledger: the time between the request and the move |
| items sent back, and why | the ledger: reject moves with their reason |
| context spent by the wiki reads and the craft skills | `/context` at the end of each stage |

These numbers decide the next fixes in the first retro.
