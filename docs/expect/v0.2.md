# Expectations for v0.2: the wiki

Status: in use, 2026-10-16. The wiki pack is built; W-03 passed on a real question. The daily brief has its scenarios in part E.
Each scenario says what you type, what you get, and what you reject. The step is done when every scenario passes on a real folder.
The learn pack and `/retro` get their scenarios in this file later.

## Decisions

| Point | Answer |
|---|---|
| The wiki | `docs/wiki/`, with one `INDEX.md` on each shelf. `docs/references/`, `work/`, `learn/`, `docs/adr/` and `archive/` are not the wiki. |
| `ideas/` | An idea that has not started: what it is, why, the open questions. `/grill-me` adds its decision to the page. |
| `areas/` | A responsibility with no end date: the standard to keep, the habits, the current goal. The owner writes it; Nook reads it. |
| `resources/` | Knowledge to look up: how a system works (`/close`), a lesson of a source (`/wiki-add`), a concept (`/study`). |
| `daily/` | One note for each day. It has no index line. |
| The index lines | Nook adds and checks the lines; the owner can edit the summary of any line, and Nook keeps that edit. |
| A source from the web | `docs/references/<date>-<slug>.md`: the link, the title, the date and the main points in our words, never a full copy. |
| Words | "The shelf `areas/`" is always named with the word shelf, so it never reads as an engineering area, which is a repo. |

## A. The wiki and its index

### W-01 Turn the wiki on
- You type: `wiki` into `packs` of `nook.yml`, then `/setup`.
- You get: `/setup` finds `docs/wiki/` with its shelves `ideas/`, `areas/`, `resources/` and `daily/`, and writes a `wiki:` section with each shelf and its use, as in Decisions. Each page and each `INDEX.md` stays as it is. `nook doctor` lists no wiki gap.
- You reject: a page that moves or gets a new name; a shelf that nobody asked for; a rewrite of an index line that you wrote.

### W-02 One cheap read first
- You type: `nook wiki index --check`.
- You get: the index of each shelf except `daily/` has one line for each page of the shelf, and each line names a page that exists. `docs/wiki/INDEX.md` lists the shelves. Each gap comes with its fix.
- You reject: an agent that opens a page before it reads the index; a page with no line.

## B. Read the wiki

### W-03 Ask the wiki
- You type: `/wiki-ask why does an empty status list every shipment?`.
- You get: an answer in a few lines with a link to each page that it used. It read the index first and opened only the pages whose line matches. With no page that matches, the answer is "not in the wiki", and the question becomes a candidate page.
- You reject: an answer from memory with no page; a page that it opened with no match in the index.

### W-04 A stage reads what applies
- You type: `/spec IT-3` for an item on the shipments page.
- You get: `/spec` reads the index, opens the pages that match the item, and names each one in the spec where it applies, as `(wiki: resources/shipments-api.md)`.
- You reject: a read of every page; a fact from a page with no link to it.

## C. Write the wiki

### W-05 Keep the lesson of a source
- You type: `/wiki-add https://…` or `/wiki-add <a file>`.
- You get: the source in `docs/references/`, and a draft page in the shelf `resources/`: the main points, one real example for each point, the gotchas, the source. You read the draft and say yes; then the page and its index line are written.
- You reject: a page with no real example; a page that copies the source; a page written before your yes.

### W-06 Close writes how the system works now
- You type: `/close IT-3`.
- You get: one change for each `ADD` or `CHANGE` criterion, on the page of its topic in the shelf `resources/`: how the system works now, the pitfalls that remain, and one History line with the item and the date. You see each change in the question round of `/close` and say yes or no.
- You reject: the story of the item on a page; a page for each item in place of a page for each topic; a change that you did not see.

### W-07 Tidy the wiki
- You type: `/wiki-tidy`.
- You get: a list of broken `[[links]]`, pages with no line in the index, lines with no page, two pages on one topic, pages with no source, and quality words with no example. The nook fixes the mechanical ones after your yes; the rest is a list for you.
- You reject: a page deleted without your yes; a merge of two pages without your yes.

## D. Boundaries

### W-08 The wiki stays yours
- You type: any wiki skill.
- You get: pages and references only in the environment that turns the wiki on. The nook repo holds the skills and the templates, never a page.
- You reject: a page of your wiki in the nook repo; a page of the company folder in the personal one.

## E. The daily brief

Decisions (2026-10-16): `/brief` is a daily habit in chat; it writes no file and no page. It covers every folder that you init. It shows the gaps of the doctor and the wiki drafts that wait, next to the items of engineering.

### BR-01 Init lists the folder
- You type: `nook init` in a folder.
- You get: the folder in your list of environments, `~/.config/nook/environments`, one path on each line. A folder that no longer exists drops off the list at the next brief.
- You reject: a folder twice in the list; a list in a folder of a project.

### BR-02 One brief for every folder
- You type: `/brief`, in any folder.
- You get, in chat: "Waits for you" first, then "Done since yesterday", then "Runs". Each line names one thing and the action for it, such as "IT-7 waits for your accept → `/gate accept IT-7`". With more than one folder, each line names its folder. The most urgent line comes first.
- You reject: a page or a file that the brief writes; a line with no action; a folder of your list that the brief skips.

### BR-03 A broken setup shows first
- You type: `/brief` while `nook doctor` finds a gap in one of your folders.
- You get: the gap under "Waits for you", with its fix, such as "personal: the wiki index lacks a line → `nook wiki index --fix`".
- You reject: a gap that only `nook doctor` shows.

### BR-04 A wiki draft waits for your yes
- You type: `/wiki-add` and you do not decide the draft in that session, then `/brief` later.
- You get: the draft kept in `docs/wiki/drafts/`, and the line "a wiki draft waits for your yes: <page>" under "Waits for you".
- You reject: a draft that is lost when the session ends; a draft that the wiki shows as a page before your yes.

### BR-05 A quiet day
- You type: `/brief` when nothing waits.
- You get: "Nothing waits for you", then what is done since yesterday and what runs.
- You reject: an empty answer.

## F. The retro

Decisions (2026-10-16): `/retro` writes `docs/retro/<date>.md` in the environment and shows the top points in chat. It covers the time since the last retro, or 7 days for the first one. The facts come from the ledger; a page that nobody read and the trend of a course wait for the learn pack and a record of reads.

### RT-01 The facts, with their source
- You type: `/retro`.
- You get: each item sent back, reopened, reframed or replanned, with its reason; each guess flipped; each blocking question that waited 30 min or more for you, or still waits; each slice that failed, and each slice whose last proof is weak or none. Each fact names its item and the event it comes from, in one line.
- You reject: a fact with no source; a fact from before the span.

### RT-02 Causes and changes
- You get: for each group of facts, the cause in one line, and the change that stops it: a rule, a skill or the nook. You pick the changes to make with one question round.
- You reject: a change with no fact behind it; a change that the agent makes before your yes.

### RT-03 One file for each retro
- You get: `docs/retro/<date>.md` with "In short", "What happened", "Causes and changes", "Kept". The chat shows the in-short line and the changes that you picked.
- You reject: a second file for the same day; a retro that edits an old one.

### RT-04 A quiet span
- You type: `/retro` after a span with no fact.
- You get: the file with "Nothing went wrong in the span" and what was kept, and no question.
- You reject: a change proposed with no fact.

## G. The learn pack

Decisions (2026-10-16): the first course is English to IELTS General, band 7 in 9 to 12 months, 45 to 60 minutes each day. The course covers Listening, Reading and Writing; speaking is out (the owner, 2026-10-15). CEFR is the map; the IELTS band descriptors are the ruler. Captures of the owner's own text are off until the roadmap turns them on. `/learn-progress` comes in v0.3.

### L-01 Set up a course
- You type: `/learn-setup english`.
- You get: one question round (the goal, the date, the minutes, the skills); then a placement for each skill, with the task that it comes from; then `learn/english/` with `roadmap.md` (the map, the goal, the level of each skill, the milestones of each month), `mistakes.md`, `scores.md`, `cards/` and `study.md`.
- You reject: a level with no task behind it; a roadmap with no date; a speaking skill.

### L-02 The day
- You type: `/learn-today`.
- You get, in the minutes of the roadmap: the cards that are due today, one lesson on your top mistake pattern with a drill, and one short task of your own output. Each mistake of the day goes into `mistakes.md` with its pattern, your sentence and the fix; a new point becomes a card.
- You reject: a lesson on a pattern that is not in your mistakes; more minutes than the roadmap allows; a mistake with no example of yours.

### L-03 Practice on the ruler
- You type: `/learn-practice english writing task 2`.
- You get: a timed task like the real test; then a score for each criterion of the ruler (Task Response, Coherence and Cohesion, Lexical Resource, Grammatical Range and Accuracy), each with the sentence of yours that it rests on; the band and the next step. The score goes into `scores.md`.
- You reject: a band with no quoted evidence; a score that the coach makes without the criteria; a score that replaces your own judgment.

### L-04 Cards come back on time
- You type: `/learn-today` on the days after a card.
- You get: a card that you know comes back later each time (1, 2, 4, 8, 16 days); a card that you miss comes back the next day.
- You reject: a card that never comes back; all the cards on one day.

### L-05 Study one topic
- You type: `/study present perfect versus past simple`.
- You get: the facts first, then you explain it, an example of yours, a check; the topic as a concept page in the wiki, for your yes, and one line in `study.md`.
- You reject: a page with no example; a page written before your yes.

### L-06 The brief knows the course
- You type: `/brief` on a day of the course.
- You get: "English: 12 cards due, no practice yet today → `/learn-today`" under "Waits for you".
- You reject: a brief that misses the cards that are due.
