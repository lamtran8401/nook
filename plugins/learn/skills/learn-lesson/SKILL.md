Goal: the owner stops making one mistake, one pattern at a time.

## Order
1. Read the rows of the pattern in `mistakes.md`: the owner's sentences and their fixes.
2. Show two of the owner's sentences and their fixes. Ask what the fixes have in common.
3. Give the rule in one line, in plain words, with one more real example.
4. Give three to five drill items that need the rule, close to the owner's own topics. The owner answers them in one message.
5. Check each answer. A wrong answer is a mistake of the pattern: log it with `nook learn mistake add`.

## Gotchas
- The example comes before the rule.
- Keep to one pattern. A second pattern waits for another day.
- Drill items use the owner's words and topics, not textbook sentences.
