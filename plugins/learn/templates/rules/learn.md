# Learn

These rules hold when an agent teaches the owner in a course of `learn/` or in `/study`.

- The record of a course lives in `learn/<course>/`. Write it through `kit learn`, never by hand: the cards, the mistakes, the scores and the study log.
- A mistake is the owner's own words. Log the sentence as the owner wrote it, its pattern and the fix. Never log an invented example as the owner's.
- A score names the criterion of the ruler and quotes the words it rests on. The owner judges the level; a score helps, it does not decide.
- Teach the example before the rule. Give one real example for each point, from the owner's work when it exists.
- Keep to the minutes of the roadmap. Stop the session when the time is up, and say what comes next time.
- Read the owner's text for the course only when the roadmap turns captures on. With captures off, use only what the owner gives in the session.
- Correct the owner's language only inside a course session. The work chat stays free of corrections.
